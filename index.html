Welcome

I am a Senior Lecturer at the School of Business Management, Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM), where I have taught and researched since 2005. My work looks at how businesses, especially franchises and small and medium enterprises, build lasting relationships, adopt technology, and stay competitive.

What I work on

My research began with franchising: what makes the relationship between franchisor and franchisee work, and how relational norms, trust and relationship quality shape loyalty and business performance. That line of work was supported by an FRGS grant on relational mechanisms in education franchise systems.

I also study information systems and technology adoption in organisations, from ICT use in rural SMEs to systems interoperability in government, and more recently the role of big data, AI and ChatGPT in business and higher education. A third strand looks at entrepreneurship among university students and the sustainability of social enterprises.

Working with me

I supervise postgraduate research in business management and welcome enquiries from prospective students, collaborators, and industry partners interested in franchising, SME development, and digital transformation. Please reach me by email or through the profiles on this page.

Research

My research sits at the meeting point of relationship marketing, information systems, and small business development. I use quantitative methods, particularly structural equation modelling (SEM and PLS-SEM), alongside conceptual and bibliometric work.

Franchise relationships

Relationship value, trust, relational norms and relationship quality as drivers of franchisee loyalty and franchise firm performance in Malaysia.

Information systems & ICT adoption

Technology adoption in rural SMEs, IS interoperability and IT capability in public organisations, and e-learning success.

Digital business & AI

Big data and competitive advantage, AI and ChatGPT in academic outcomes, and geospatial methods for customer lifetime value.

Entrepreneurship & SMEs

Entrepreneurial intention and cognition among students, green and low-carbon practices in SMEs, and social enterprise sustainability.
